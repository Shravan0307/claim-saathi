# Claim Saathi

Build a polished, realistic hackathon MVP web application called:

CLAIM SAATHI

Tagline:

"Helping families navigate financial closure after a loss."

IMPORTANT:

This is a software hackathon prototype for ENIGMA 5.0 — FinTech track.

The product is NOT a banking app, insurance company, legal service, or financial institution.

It is a digital coordination and guidance layer that helps a grieving family discover, organize, understand, and track financial closure tasks after the death of a family member.

The prototype must feel like a real Indian fintech product, NOT like an AI-generated generic dashboard.

==================================================

CORE PRODUCT IDEA

==================================================

After a family member dies, financial information and claim processes are fragmented across banks, EPFO, insurance, investments, shares, loans and government portals.

The family may not even know which assets exist, which documents are required, who the nominee is, what needs to be done first, or which institution to contact.

CLAIM SAATHI creates one organized financial estate map and converts it into a prioritized action plan.

Core workflow:

DISCOVER → ORGANIZE → VERIFY → ACT → TRACK

The system should support:

1. Guided estate intake / triage

2. Financial asset discovery

3. Asset and liability inventory

4. 30-day action playbook

5. Claim/document checklist

6. Liability and EMI notification support

7. Unclaimed asset discovery guidance

8. Claim status tracking

9. Document organization

10. Clear explanations of complex financial/legal terminology

Do NOT claim that the app directly accesses bank, EPFO, insurance or government databases.

For the prototype, use sample/mock data and clearly label integrations as "Guided" or "Coming in future".

IMPORTANT PRODUCT BOUNDARIES

==================================================

Never say:

"Claim Saathi automatically finds all bank accounts."

Never say:

"Claim Saathi automatically files claims."

Never say:

"Claim Saathi guarantees claim approval."

Never provide legal advice as fact.

Instead position the product as:

"AI-assisted organization and workflow guidance."

The core differentiator is:

Existing systems solve individual institutional processes.

CLAIM SAATHI connects the family's entire post-death financial closure journey into one workflow.

The product should feel like:

"one calm control center during a chaotic financial process."
